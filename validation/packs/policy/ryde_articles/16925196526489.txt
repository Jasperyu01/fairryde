Can a driver terminate a ride after the rider has already boarded the vehicle? If so, what are the circumstances under which a driver may terminate a ride in progress?

The driver may terminate a ride in progress if there is a safety concern or if the rider is violating the community guidelines set by the platform. This could include situations where the rider is verbally or physically abusive towards the driver, is intoxicated or engaging in illegal activities, or is not following the driver's instructions for safe and proper use of the vehicle. In such cases, the driver is permitted to end the ride and ask the rider to exit the vehicle immediately. It's important for both drivers and riders to follow the platform's guidelines to ensure a safe and positive experience for everyone involved. Include vehicle breakdown.

Additionally, a driver may also terminate a ride in progress if their vehicle breaks down or malfunctions, and they are unable to safely continue the ride. In such cases, the driver should do their best to safely pull over to the side of the road and inform the rider of the situation. It's important for drivers to regularly maintain their vehicles to minimize the risk of breakdowns and ensure a safe and reliable ride for their passengers.
