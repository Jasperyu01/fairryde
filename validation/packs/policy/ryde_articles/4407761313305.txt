RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 4407761313305
TITLE: Does the Cancellation Policy apply to all Ryde services?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407761313305-Does-the-Cancellation-Policy-apply-to-all-Ryde-services
EDITED: 2024-03-18

Cancellation Policy applies to all Service Types * in the Ryde app and is executed on a service level, not driver level. Hence, all carpool drivers are included in the Cancellation Policy.

*All services except RydeTAXI
