RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 115000772593
TITLE: My Ryde driver is uncontactable. What should I do?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Ryde Experience
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000772593-My-Ryde-driver-is-uncontactable-What-should-I-do
EDITED: 2024-05-28

You may like to try contacting your Ryde driver via the in-app chat or via a call. If he/she remains uncontactable, you will probably have to cancel the trip and request for another ride.

We are sorry if this happens to you as we understand how disappointed you must be. At Ryde, we do our best to constantly monitor the errant behaviours of Ryde drivers to improve Rider Experiences.
