What is the Service Fee?

The service fee, also known as the commission fee, supports the operations and continuous development of our platform. It helps us enhance app performance, introduce new features, and expand our services to better meet the needs our consumers.

0% Commission, 100% Support for PHV and Taxi Drivers

This initiative reflects our dedication to supporting driver-partners and ensuring they receive the maximum value for their work. As part of our commitment to building a fairer and more rewarding platform, we introduced our 0% commission initiative in mid-2023.

Under this initiative:

- No commission fees for PHV and taxi drivers until 31 December 2027
- Drivers keep 100% of their fare earnings
- Whether driving full-time or part-time, you stay in full control of your trips and income

* A 10% service fee applies to Carpool Drivers

Tiered Platform Fee Structure

We have implemented a tiered platform fee, which is charged to riders and included as part of the base fare. This fee helps maintain and enhance the Ryde platform experience.

Fee Structure
*First Tier Platform Fee: $1.25 (including GST) for fares $18 and below.
*Second Tier Platform Fee: $1.47 (including GST) for fares above $18.
The platform fees are applicable for both cash and cashless payments
