Our 7 Driver Commitments to Driver-Partners

0% COMMISSION, BUILDING A FAIRER PLATFORM

At Ryde, we aim to empower our driver-partners on every journey. Our 7 Commitments serve as guiding principles, shaping how we support and empower our driver-partners. Along with our groundbreaking 0% Commission, these commitments highlight our dedication to creating a fairer platform for everyone.

Ryde's 7 Driver Commitments include:

1. Fair Earnings
We prioritise transparent pricing with more earning opportunities and we provide the lowest fees in the market.

2. Safety First
We prioritise driver-partner safety across all aspects of our platform and ensure a secure and reliable driving experience.

3. Professional Development
We are dedicated to upskilling our driver-partners through resources and continuous training

4. Dedicated Support
We offer multiple support channels for driver-partners and provide comprehensive responses via real-time chat support.

5. Job Flexibility
We empower driver-partners to choose jobs, set working hours, and work on their own terms for both part-time and full time drivers.

6. Active Engagement
We actively listen to our driver-partners' feedback and recognise their contribution through various online channels, and offline events.

7. Sustainability
We focus on sustainable transportation solutions by promoting eco-friendly practices such as RydePOOL, and multi-stops feature to reduce our carbon footprint

For more details, please visit: https://rydesharing.com/7-driver-commitments/
