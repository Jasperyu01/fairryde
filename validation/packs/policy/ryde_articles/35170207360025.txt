RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 35170207360025
TITLE: Driver profile did not match on app
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Ryde Experience
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/35170207360025-Driver-profile-did-not-match-on-app
EDITED: 2024-07-17

If you have been picked up by a driver different from the driver profile on our app or from a different car, you may choose not to enter the vehicle and arrange for alternative transport in the app. Please raise a ticket via Rydehelp on your app with photo evidence.
