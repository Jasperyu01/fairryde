RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 4408080441497
TITLE: Do I need to pay an extra fee for my bulky items or wheelchair?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408080441497-Do-I-need-to-pay-an-extra-fee-for-my-bulky-items-or-wheelchair
EDITED: 2024-10-01

As of 18 November 2021, there will be no surcharge for bulky items or wheelchairs. However, we strongly encourage that you leave a note in both 'Special Request' and 'Note to Driver' when you book the trip.

You may be liable for cancellation fees if you need to cancel the trip due to the lack of space. Please refer to our cancellation policy here.
