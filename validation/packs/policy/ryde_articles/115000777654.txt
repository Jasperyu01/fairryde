TITLE: How do I make carpool experience more enjoyable as a rider?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: RydePOOL
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000777654-How-do-I-make-carpool-experience-more-enjoyable-as-a-rider
EDITED: 2023-05-05

Adhere to our Code of Conduct at all times so we can all make our carpool community a safer and better one.

Be Punctual - We all have important appointments to keep.

Communicate - Be clear about location, date and timing. Confirm with a call if needed!

Commitment - Keep to your words. Be considerate!

Respect - Show mutual respect. Don't eat, drink or smoke without the driver's permission in the car.

Safety First - Abide by traffic laws. Buckle up and alight at a safe location!
