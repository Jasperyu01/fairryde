TITLE: What if I booked the wrong pick-up point?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Popular Topics
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/50736334641561-What-if-I-booked-the-wrong-pick-up-point
EDITED: 2025-09-16

- Check your pick-up location before confirming the ride.
- If the pick-up point is incorrect, re-enter the correct address.
- If you realize the pick-up is wrong after being matched with a driver, please cancel and rebook. Standard cancellation fees may apply.
