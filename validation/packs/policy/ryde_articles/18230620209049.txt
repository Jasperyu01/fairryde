TITLE: How do I tip my driver?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/18230620209049-How-do-I-tip-my-driver
EDITED: 2026-09-10

Tipping is currently available within 2 hours after the trip is completed. If the tipping window has closed, the "Tip" option will no longer be available.

For future trips, you can tip your driver during or at the end of the trip through Trip Details.

1. Open Trip Details - Tap or swipe up on the bottom sheet while your trip is active or after it ends.

2. Tap "Tip" - Scroll to the section at the bottom of the Trip Details card and select Tip.

3. Select an Amount - Choose a preset tip or enter a custom amount.

4. Confirm - Tap Confirm to complete the transaction.
