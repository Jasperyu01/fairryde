TITLE: How do I make a RydeX booking?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: RydeX & XL
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004108473-How-do-I-make-a-RydeX-booking
EDITED: 2024-10-08

To make a RydeX booking, simply follow the below 6 steps.

1. Tap on RydeX icon
2. Enter Pick-up / Drop-off location along with pickup date or time.
3. You can book advance trips up to 7 days in advance.
4. Choose to pay with cash or go cashless with RydePay.
5. Tap Book
6. Wait for a driver to accept.
7. You can track a driver on the way, and yourself during the trip.
