RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 115000772653
TITLE: Do I need to pay ERP charges?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000772653-Do-I-need-to-pay-ERP-charges
EDITED: 2024-04-19

If a rider passes through an active ERP gantry, the toll charge will be applicable to the rider.

For all services apart from RydeSEND, ERP charges are excluded from the fares.

*RydeSEND fares include ERP charges.
