TITLE: Jurong Island Entry Pass
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges

EDITED: 2025-07-17

Riders are advised to plan accordingly, as further transport arrangements may be required beyond the entry point.

Drivers are not permitted to enter Jurong Island unless they have a dedicated Jurong Island Entry Pass.

Drivers can pick up or drop off riders at the designated Pass Registration Office carpark located outside the Jurong Island entrance.
