TITLE: How do I complete the trip in the Ryde app?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Accepting a Ryde
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001439693-How-do-I-complete-the-trip-in-the-Ryde-app
EDITED: 2023-06-16

1. Once you have completed the rider's trip, tap DROP OFF.
2. Enter the Toll (ERP) charge, if incurred.
3. Rate your Rider.
4. Input Reviews.
5. Tap Confirm to end the trip.

Always remember to provide an honest rating and review for the rider.

Please also bear in mind that if an additional / excessive amount is keyed in for Toll (ERP), this will be considered fraudulent and drivers will be liable for permanent suspension.
