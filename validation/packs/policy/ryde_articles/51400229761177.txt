TITLE: What is the process for a driver accepting the edit a destination request from Rider?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Add/Edit Destination
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/51400229761177-What-is-the-process-for-a-driver-accepting-the-edit-a-destination-request-from-Rider
EDITED: 2025-10-10

1. The driver receives an edit request pop-up.
2. The driver can accept or reject the request.
3. If the driver accepts, they receive a notification, and the add/edit destination fee is reflected on their receipt.
4. If the driver rejects, the previous trip details remain, and no fee is reflected on their receipt.
