My live tracking is not working

Please ensure to always open your app to receive the live tracking. If you failed to get the live tracking, it might be because your driver/recipient did not turn on his/her app thus you may advise them to open the app. If the issue persists, do let us know by providing more details with a screenshot or screen recording.

My driver is moving further away or not moving

My app displays the arrival time of my driver. But the map shows that the driver is not moving or moving further. Do let us know by providing more details with a screenshot or screen recording.
