How to top up your RydeCoins

1. Tap on the Wallet on the top right corner.
2. Choose Top-Up.
3. Choose your value and top-up method.
4. Tap Top-Up when completed.
5. Your available coins will be displayed. You can now choose to pay with RydeCoins!

Can I refund my RydeCoins?

Like a prepaid mobile plan, RydeCoins are non-refundable and not redeemable, exchanged or transferred for cash value under any circumstances.

Will my RydeCoins expire?

Your coins are valid for 6 months from your last wallet transaction.

How much RydeCoins can I top up each time?

There is a top up limit of $1,000 per transaction.

What is the minimum amount I can top-up in my RydeCoins wallet?

The minimum amount needed for a top-up is $2.

What is the maximum amount I can have in my RydeCoins wallet?

Your RydeCoins wallet should hold a maximum of $1,000 at any one time.

Can RydeCoins be combined with other payment methods for any rides?

No. Payment has to be made in full using RydeCoins.

What does "On-Hold" mean in my transaction history?

Your Rydecoins are held for an upcoming ride and will be deducted after a trip is completed. No coins will be deducted if the ride is cancelled.

Can I use RydeCoins overseas?

No, your RydeCoins will only be valid locally.
