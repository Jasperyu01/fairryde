TITLE: What is the process for adding/editing a destination after a trip has started?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Add/Edit Destination Fee
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/51399674412441-What-is-the-process-for-adding-editing-a-destination-after-a-trip-has-started
EDITED: 2025-10-10

1. The trip has started.
2. The rider needs to let their driver know.
3. The destination is selected.
4. The rider types and selects the new destination.
5. There will be a map confirmation (for a single stop).
6. The rider can add another destination, leading to a map confirmation (for multiple stops).
7. New destination and fare confirmation will be displayed.
8. The rider waits for the driver to accept or reject the request.
9. If the driver accepts, the trip details are updated, and the add/edit destination fee is reflected on the rider receipt.
10. If the driver rejects, the previous trip details remain.
