Seat belts are a mandatory requirement. Both you and your driver must wear them throughout the ride to ensure safety.

If the seat belt at your seat is damaged or if your driver isn't wearing one, let us know immediately. Your safety matters, and we're here to ensure every ride is safe and comfortable.

How to report a seat belt issue

- To report a seat belt issue, follow these steps:
- Open the Ryde app and go to RydeHELP.
- Select Report a Safety Issue.
- Fill in the details of the issue, including the vehicle's registration number and any other relevant information.
- Submit the form, and our team will investigate promptly.

Your vigilance helps us maintain a safe and reliable experience for all. If you need any more help, please contact our support team via RydeHELP.
