TITLE: How to add ERP to my trip fare?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Fares & Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900005170163-How-to-add-ERP-to-my-trip-fare
EDITED: 2023-06-25

How to input Toll (ERP) amount:

1. Remember the amount of ERP incurred during the trip.
2. Tap on Drop Off.
3. Enter the amount in the box next to Toll (ERP).
4. Rate your Rider.
5. Input Reviews.
6. Tap Confirm to end the trip.

Please bear in mind that if an additional / excessive amount is keyed in, this will be considered fraudulent and drivers will be liable for permanent suspension.
