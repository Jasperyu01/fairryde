RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 900003406966
TITLE: What are the booking fees?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: RydeTAXI
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900003406966-What-are-the-booking-fees
EDITED: 2026-01-26

When you book for RydeTAXI, the flag down fare and regular booking fees are applicable. The actual charges vary among different taxi companies.

Do note that all fees for Standard and/or Premium Taxi Services follow that of the respective taxi company.

The platform fee is included in the booking fee.

Please do note that the payment method accepted for RydeTAXI is only via cash.
