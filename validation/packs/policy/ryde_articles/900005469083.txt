RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 900005469083
TITLE: Can I pick up Riders with children?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Accepting a Ryde
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900005469083-Can-I-pick-up-Riders-with-children
EDITED: 2026-09-03

You can pick up Riders travelling with children if they have their own appropriate child restraint or booster seat. Please note that there will be no surcharges.

Below are the different scenarios to take note of:

Passengers With Children Above 1.35m

No child restraint or booster seat is required.
The child is tall enough to ride in a normal car seat while using the seat belt.

Passengers With Children Below 1.35m

Child restraint or booster seat is required.
If the passenger is able to provide the appropriate child restraint device, you may pick up the passenger. Do not add any surcharge.
If the rider does not have the required child restraint or booster seat, please cancel the trip and select "Rider is with a child" as the cancellation reason. Please contact our support team and provide us with the trip details. After verification, our team will revert the cancellation rate where applicable.

*Baby carriers/harnesses is not an appropriate child restraint device by the LTA.

If you can't accept the ride due to lack of appropriate child restraint or booster seat, please cancel the trip and indicate Rider is with a child as the reason.
