1. What is RydeX GO?

RydeX GO is a 4-seater service that prioritises nearby eligible private-hire drivers. This gives drivers more opportunities to receive trips from riders nearby.

2. Who is eligible to receive RydeX GO jobs?

RydeX GO jobs are available to eligible private hire drivers.

3. Do I need to sign up for RydeX GO?

No. Eligible drivers will automatically be able to receive RydeX GO jobs. No additional sign-up is required.

4. How will I receive RydeX GO jobs?

Eligible drivers can receive RydeX GO jobs through job ping and job list, similar to other eligible service types. Availability will depend on rider demand and driver availability.
