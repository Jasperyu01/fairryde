TITLE: Why should I use RydeX?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: RydeX & XL
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004075214-Why-should-I-use-RydeX
EDITED: 2024-10-08

RydeX offers an alternative mobility option for point to point travel. You will be matched with either a private-hire driver or a taxi driver. All fares are fixed at the time of booking but does not include toll charges such as ERP.
