TITLE: How to top up via voucher code?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/49158925783961-How-to-top-up-via-voucher-code
EDITED: 2025-07-24

1. Tap on the Wallet on the top right corner.
2. Choose Top-Up via voucher code.
3. Enter the 10 digit code.
4. Tap Top-Up when completed.
5. Your available coins will be displayed.
6. You can now choose to pay with RydeCoins!
