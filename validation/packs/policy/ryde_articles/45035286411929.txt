TITLE: How does the service comply with LTA regulations?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Carpooling
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/45035286411929-How-does-the-service-comply-with-LTA-regulations
EDITED: 2025-05-06

Our carpool service complies with the RTA (Car Pools) (Exemption) Order 2021 and the LTA P2P regulatory framework introduced in October 2020. These regulations govern cost recovery, fare structure, safety, and operational standards for carpool services. Carpool drivers are permitted to recover their operating costs in line with the framework. We continuously adapt to evolving policies to ensure our service remains legally compliant and reliable.
