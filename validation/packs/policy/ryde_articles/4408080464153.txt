TITLE: What if my bulky items (Eg. Wheelchair, luggage, etc.) are unable to fit into the vehicle?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Booking a Ryde
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408080464153-What-if-my-bulky-items-Eg-Wheelchair-luggage-etc-are-unable-to-fit-into-the-vehicle
EDITED: 2026-09-25

You will need to cancel the trip and rebook a vehicle with a larger capacity, such as RydeXL. To avoid unnecessary cancellation fees, we recommend that you leave a note in both 'Special Request' and 'Note to Driver' when you book the trip. Once you get a driver match, do remind your driver again of your request.
