TITLE: What is the difference between RydeX and RydeXL?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: RydeX & XL
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/900004157486-What-is-the-difference-between-RydeX-and-RydeXL
EDITED: 2023-03-17

At Ryde, riders can choose from several Private Hire trip options depending on their needs.

RydeX (up to 4 pax)
Private Hire Bookings. Fares vary with peak period timing. No sharing.

RydeXL (up to 6 pax)
Private Hire Bookings. Fares higher than RydeX. Fares vary with peak period timing. No sharing.
