TITLE: How is the Payment Transaction Fee calculated?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/52596204611993-How-is-the-Payment-Transaction-Fee-calculated
EDITED: 2025-11-21

The fee is based on card type and will be shown as "Transaction Fee" on your ride receipt.

- 1.96% + $0.24 for Local Visa, Mastercard and Rydecoins
- 3.3% + $0.50 for AMEX, Foreign Cards & Others
- Additional 3% applies to all Foreign Debit/Credit Cards & Alipay on top of the prevailing transaction fee
