RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 4408072389913
TITLE: Are surcharges included in the base fee?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Fares & Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408072389913-Are-surcharges-included-in-the-base-fee
EDITED: 2023-12-31

Surcharges such as toll charges, overtime fees and valet parking fees* are excluded from the base fare. Please remember to charge such additional fees at the end of the trip.

*Toll charges are already included in RydeSEND trip fares.

*For shared RydePOOL trips, each rider pays 50% of the total ERP charges.
