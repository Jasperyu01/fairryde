TITLE: Handling Trips with the Visually Impaired and their Guide Dogs
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Accepting a Ryde
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406078531097-Handling-Trips-with-the-Visually-Impaired-and-their-Guide-Dogs
EDITED: 2026-08-05

Please note that guide dogs are accepted on all Ryde services. We encourage our Driver-Partners to accept trips from riders with guide dogs if you have no religious or health concerns.

Guide Dogs can help change the lives of their visually impaired handler, giving them mobility aid to navigate daily environments dynamically, be active in the community, explore the world, and pursue personal interests and growth. As fellow Singaporeans, we can do our part to be aware of these things, or even show our support for these amazingly obedient dogs and the special bond they have with their blind handlers.

The Islamic Religious Council of Singapore (Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura) supports the use of guide dogs.
You may read the advisory here: https://www.muis.gov.sg/resources/khutbah-and-religious-advice/irsyad/advisory-on-guide-dogs-english/
