Sentosa Entrance Fee

As a rider, if you're entering Sentosa Island, you may have to pay an admission fee depending on the time of day.

The fees are as follows:
- 7:00 AM to 12:00 PM: $6*
- 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM: $2*
- 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM: $6*
- 5:00 PM to 7:00 AM: $2*

The cost of the admission fee will be charged to the rider.

Note the following scenarios where you are NOT required to pay the admission fee:

1. You have documents to waive entrance fees, such as:
- Sentosa Staff Pass
- Sentosa Islander membership
- Hotel complimentary pass

In these scenarios, the driver can use the manned entry lanes to waive the island admission fee.
