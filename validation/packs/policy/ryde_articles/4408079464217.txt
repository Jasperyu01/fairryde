TITLE: What if I forgot to add in the Waiting Time Fee?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Fares & Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408079464217-What-if-I-forgot-to-add-in-the-Waiting-Time-Fee
EDITED: 2026-09-22

Ryde will not be held accountable for the reimbursement of waiting time fees. Drivers are advised to tap on the waiting time fee toggle upon drop-off as we are unable to charge the rider once the trip is completed.
