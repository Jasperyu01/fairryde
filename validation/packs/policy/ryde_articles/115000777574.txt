RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 115000777574
TITLE: How can I pay my Ryde driver?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000777574-How-can-I-pay-my-Ryde-driver
EDITED: 2025-09-02

You can pay for your trips via Cash or RydePay (Credit/debit card or RydeCoins)! Please be sure to confirm your payment method when making a trip booking as this cannot be changed after the trip match.

Go cashless with RydePay for a seamless experience.

Please do note that the payment method accepted for RydeTAXI is only via cash.

Why is Cash not available as a payment method?
Please proceed to book with RydePay. The cash option may be temporarily disabled based on our security algorithms.

*Note: Please be reminded not to pay cash to the driver if you have chosen RydePay as your payment method.
