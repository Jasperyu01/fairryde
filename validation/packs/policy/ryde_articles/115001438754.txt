TITLE: I am unable to contact my rider. The rider did not turn up. What should I do?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Ryde Experience
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001438754-I-am-unable-to-contact-my-rider-The-rider-did-not-turn-up-What-should-I-do
EDITED: 2021-09-20

Try to contact the rider by in-app chat or call. If the rider still cannot be reached, you may need to cancel the trip.

Do not worry about rider no-shows, as our driver CR requirements have catered for a buffer based on statistical data for these unfortunate events. Drivers' CR is improved based on the most recent trips as well.
