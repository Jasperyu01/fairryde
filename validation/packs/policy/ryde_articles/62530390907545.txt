1. What is RydeX GO?
RydeX GO is a 4-seater rider option that prioritises eligible private hire drivers who are closer to your pick-up location, helping you get matched sooner.

2. How is RydeX GO different from RydeX?
Both offer 4-seater rides. RydeX GO prioritises nearby eligible private hire drivers, which may help you get matched sooner.

3. How many passengers can RydeX GO accommodate?
RydeX GO can accommodate up to 4 passengers.

4. Can I cancel a RydeX GO booking?
Yes. You can cancel your RydeX GO booking. Any applicable cancellation or waiting fees will apply in accordance with the relevant policy.

5. Will I get a premium vehicle with RydeX GO?

RydeX GO uses the same 4-seater vehicle type as RydeX. The difference is in how your ride is matched, RydeX GO prioritises nearby eligible private hire drivers.
