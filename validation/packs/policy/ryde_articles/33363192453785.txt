TITLE: What is the difference between RydePOOL, RydeFLASH, RydePET and RydeX?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Accepting a Ryde
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33363192453785-What-is-the-difference-between-RydePOOL-RydeFLASH-RydePET-and-RydeX
EDITED: 2026-09-02

Carpooling is all about sharing rides and going green! Riders can choose from several trip options depending on their needs.

RydePOOL (1 pax only)**

Riders pay for a seat.

The tolls for RydePOOL are split equally between riders for a shared trip, with each paying 50% of the total toll.

Go Green by Carpooling with RydePOOL! 🌱🚗

RydePOOL is a unique carpooling service where you share rides only with passengers heading in the same direction.

- Trip stacking is limited to a maximum of 2 pickups per ride.
- Maximum passengers per booking: 1 pax only.
- Drivers may bring 1 friend or family member to sit in the front seat during a RydePOOL ride.
- This policy applies exclusively to RydePOOL only.

RydeFLASH (Up to 4 pax)**

Available to Carpool, Private Hire and Taxi drivers

RydePET (up to 4 pax, excluding pets)**

Available to Private Hire and Taxi drivers.
Up to 3 riders and

i) a maximum of 1 small-sized pet

Up to 2 riders and

i) a maximum of 2 medium-sized pets or

ii) 1 large-sized pet

E.g. 1 Maltese and 1 Cat or only 1 Golden Retriever.

(Any other arrangements are at the driver's discretion)

RydeX (up to 4 pax)**

Private Hire Bookings. Fares may vary with peak period timing. No sharing.

For Carpool trips:

- The total ERP charge is shared equally between the driver and passenger.
- The driver bears 50% of the ERP charge, and the passenger pays the remaining 50%.

** The fares for RydeFLASH / RydePET / RydeX / RydeXL / RydeLUXE / RydeTAXI exclude ERP.
