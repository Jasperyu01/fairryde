TITLE: Who bears financial costs in the event of an accident?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: (not returned in sections listing)
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001439653-Who-bears-financial-costs-in-the-event-of-an-accident
EDITED: 2024-06-06

In the case of an unfortunate event, a driver's vehicle and personal/commercial insurance should cover the costs incurred from the accident. Riders may file a medical claim through the driver's insurance.
