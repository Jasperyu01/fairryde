TITLE: Can I charge an extra fee for bulky items or a wheelchair?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Fares & Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408117287577-Can-I-charge-an-extra-fee-for-bulky-items-or-a-wheelchair
EDITED: 2023-06-16

Please note that as of 18 November 2021, there will be no surcharge for bulky items. We recommend that you accept the trip only if your vehicle has enough additional space.
