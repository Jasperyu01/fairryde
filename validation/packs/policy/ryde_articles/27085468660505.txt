TITLE: Waiting Fee Waiver Request
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/27085468660505-Waiting-Fee-Waiver-Request
EDITED: 2026-09-25

If you feel that you have been unfairly charged for a Waiting Fee, you may submit a fee waiver request via RydeHELP in our app within 30 days of the trip* and we will review your eligibility for a refund.

If you are eligible, we will issue you a voucher** with a unique code worth $5.00 as a refund for the incurred Waiting Charges. Please be mindful to top up your Ryde wallet using the voucher code given to you within the expiry period.

*Requests submitted after 30 days of the trip will not be considered for waivers.

**This voucher is non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash in part or in full. There is strictly no extension of the expiry date from the date of issue.
