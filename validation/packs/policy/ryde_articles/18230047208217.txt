RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 18230047208217
TITLE: Can I bring along bulky items? Eg. Wheelchair, luggage, etc.
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Booking a Ryde
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/18230047208217-Can-I-bring-along-bulky-items-Eg-Wheelchair-luggage-etc
EDITED: 2024-06-06

Yes, you may. However, this is subject to the available space in the driver's car. Please leave a note in both 'Special Request' and 'Note to Driver' when you book the trip. Once you get a driver match, do remind your driver again of your request.
