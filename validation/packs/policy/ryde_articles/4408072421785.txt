RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 4408072421785
TITLE: Are surcharges included in the base fee?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408072421785-Are-surcharges-included-in-the-base-fee
EDITED: 2023-04-13

Surcharges such as toll charges, overtime fees and valet parking fees* are excluded from the base fare. You will be charged for such additional fees at the end of the trip, on top of your base fee.

*Toll charges are already included in RydeHIRE and RydeSEND trip fares.
*Overtime fees and valet parking fees are applicable for RydeHIRE trips only.
