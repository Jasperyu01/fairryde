TITLE: What should I know as a Ryde driver?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Accepting a Ryde

EDITED: 2026-01-07

Adhere to our Code of Conduct so we can all make our carpool community a safer and better one.

Be Punctual - We all have important appointments to keep.

Communicate - Be clear about location, date and timing. Confirm with a call if needed!

Commitment - Keep to your words. Be considerate! Complete the job in a timely manner.

Respect - Show mutual respect. Don't eat, drink or smoke when a rider is in the car.

Safety First - Abide by traffic laws. Drive safely and buckle up!
