TITLE: Transaction Fee
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges

EDITED: 2025-11-23

We have introduced a transaction fee for cashless payments effective 2 January 2024.

It's a small fee applied when you pay by card or other cashless methods, covering the cost of secure payment processing charged by the payment provider.

Fee structure wef 4 Dec
1.96% + $0.24 for Local Visa, Mastercard, and Rydecoins
3.3% + $0.50 for AMEX, Foreign Cards & Other credit/debit cards
An additional 3% applies to all Foreign Debit/Credit Cards & Alipay on top of the prevailing transaction fee
