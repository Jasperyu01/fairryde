RYDE HELP CENTRE ARTICLE 52595834493593
TITLE: Is there a fee for cashless transactions on Ryde?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Fares and Charges
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/52595834493593-Is-there-a-fee-for-cashless-transactions-on-Ryde
EDITED: 2025-11-23

Yes. A Transaction Fee applies to all cashless payments (e.g., cards, digital wallets, RydeCoins) to cover payment processing costs. Cash payments are not subject to this fee.
