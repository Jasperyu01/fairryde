TITLE: What if my driver hasn't arrived, but has indicated that he/she arrived?
AUDIENCE: Rider
SECTION: Ryde Experience
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407754403353-What-if-my-driver-hasn-t-arrived-but-has-indicated-that-he-she-arrived
EDITED: 2026-02-26

Drivers are only allowed to indicate arrival and impose the Waiting Time Fee only if he/she:

1. Is at the pick-up location according to GPS data AND
2. Has waited for more than 3 mins

Otherwise, you will not be charged the $6.61 Waiting Time Fee if any of the above are not fulfilled.

Nonetheless, do reach out to us if you feel you have been charged unfairly or wrongly, and we'll be happy to assist you.
