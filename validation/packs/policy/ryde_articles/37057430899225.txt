We offer two types of seats to ensure a safe and comfortable ride for your little ones:

Baby Seat:
For infants and toddlers, we offer one Baby Seat for an additional $3.

Child Seat:
For older children who require a bit more support, we provide one Child Booster Seat for an additional $5

How can I add a baby or child seat to my ride?
If you would like to add one of these options to your ride, simply tap on the three dots found on the right side on the booking screen to add the option.

Why should I choose these seat options?
These seat options are designed to ensure the safety and comfort of your child during the ride. We prioritize your family's well-being, and these seats help provide the necessary support for different age groups.

*According to the Road Traffic Act (Chapter 276, Clause 8), when travelling in a private hire vehicle, a passenger below 1.35 metres in height must be properly secured by:
1. An approved child restraint appropriate for a person of that height and weight; or
2. A body-restraining seat belt when seated on a booster seat cushion or when using a seat with an approved adjustable seat belt.
