TITLE: What if my Rider rates me poorly because of the Cancellation or Waiting Time Fee?
AUDIENCE: Driver
SECTION: Ryde Experience
URL: https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408079364889-What-if-my-Rider-rates-me-poorly-because-of-the-Cancellation-or-Waiting-Time-Fee
EDITED: 2023-02-17

If you feel that you have been unfairly rated due to the additional fees imposed on riders, please write in using the RydeHELP feature on your app to submit your appeal. Based on our Ratings and Review Moderation Policy, the team will review your appeal and consider removing ratings that are irrelevant to the driver's quality of service.
