## Ryde policy and company

### Claim 1. Rider cancellation and no-show policy; S$6.61 vs S$5; driver S$4.50

Verdict: CONFIRMED for every element as described in note 01. Note 06's wording is CORRECTED. Ryde's own driver pages CONFLICT on the driver-side waiting fee.

Rider policy, "Cancellation and Waiting Fee" (created 30 Sep 2023, edited 26 Feb 2026): https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/23534478435353-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Fee
- On-demand: "All cancellations made within 3 minutes of matching are not chargeable."
- "Rider will be charged a Cancellation Fee of $6.61* if: Rider cancels after 3 minutes of matching OR The driver presses "I'm Here", waits for > 3 minutes for the rider to arrive at the pick-up point and cancels the trip (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion)"
- "Riders may be charged a Waiting Time Fee of $5 at the end of the trip if: The driver presses "I'm Here", waits for > 3 minutes for the rider to board, and continues the trip (Rider boards and driver completes trip)."
- Advance: "All cancellations made > 15 minutes before the scheduled trip time are not chargeable." "If the advanced trip request is accepted only after 5:45 PM, riders will have a 3 minute grace period". "Rider will be charged a Cancellation Fee of $6.61* if: Rider cancels after the cancellation window (< Scheduled - 15mins OR Matched) OR The driver cancels the trip after waiting for > 3 minutes for the Rider to arrive at the pick-up point (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion)"

GPS condition, "What if my driver hasn't arrived, but has indicated that he/she arrived?" (edited 26 Feb 2026): https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407754403353
- "Drivers are only allowed to indicate arrival and impose the Waiting Time Fee only if he/she: 1. Is at the pick-up location according to GPS data AND 2. Has waited for more than 3 mins. Otherwise, you will not be charged the $6.61 Waiting Time Fee if any of the above are not fulfilled."
- Note: this article calls S$6.61 a "Waiting Time Fee", which is inconsistent with the main policy's labels.

Fee goes to the driver:
- Driver policy, "Cancellation and Waiting Time Policy" (created 3 Jul 2023, edited 23 Apr 2024): https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244064619161-Cancellation-and-Waiting-Time-Policy : "A Cancellation Fee compensates drivers for the time and effort they spend getting to the rider's location." "A Waiting Time Fee allows our drivers to be compensated by riders for the time spent waiting at a pickup location."
- Cash trips (edited 4 May 2023): https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408070775193 : "the Cancellation or Waiting Time Fee will be deducted from your RydeCoins wallet and credited directly to the driver's wallet."

Driver amounts:
- Same driver policy (edited 23 Apr 2024): "Driver will receive a Cancellation Fee of $4.50* if: 1. Rider cancels after 3 minutes of matching". "Driver may charge a Waiting Time Fee of $4.50 at the end of the trip if: 1. The driver pressed "I'm Here", waits for > 3 minutes for rider to arrive at pick-up point and cancels the trip (Rider NO SHOW and no trip completion), OR 2. The driver pressed "I'm Here", waits for > 3 minutes for rider to board, and continues the trip".
- "How Does the Cancellation Fee Work?" (edited 25 Sep 2026, so the S$4.50 figure is not only on 2024 pages): https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408070838297 : "Drivers will receive a $4.50 cancellation fee automatically if they cancel after >3 minutes of waiting."
- "How Does the Waiting Fee Work?" (edited 25 Sep 2026): https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408071347993 : "Drivers are required to tap on the $5* Waiting Time Fee button upon Drop-off (if applicable)."

Resolution for the report:
- What the rider pays: S$6.61 when the rider cancels more than 3 minutes after matching (or after the advance-booking window), or when the driver cancels as a no-show after pressing "I'm Here", being at the pickup per GPS and waiting more than 3 minutes. S$5 only as a waiting fee when the driver waited more than 3 minutes and the rider then boarded and the trip was completed.
- What the driver receives: driver pages say S$4.50 for a cancellation (2024 policy and the 25 Sep 2026 article). For the waiting fee the driver pages CONFLICT: S$4.50 in the 2024 policy versus a "$5*" button in the 25 Sep 2026 article.
- Note 06 (lines 28, 91 and its rule profile `NO_SHOW_FEE_SGD: 4.50`) takes "$4.50 after 3 minutes" from the driver policy and calls it "current ... no date shown". That page was last edited 23 Apr 2024, and S$4.50 is the driver-side amount, not the rider charge. For the rider charge use S$6.61 (no-show or late cancel) and S$5 (waiting fee).

### Claim 4. Driver thresholds, fraud definition, dashcam rule

Verdict: CONFIRMED.

Thresholds, "Driver Performance and Community Standards": Popular Topics version (edited 22 May 2026) https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/20244107265689 and Community Guidelines version (edited 10 Apr 2026) https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362721809049. Both have, under "Conditions, where driver accounts will be temporarily disabled": "Driver Rating (DR) | < 4.80" and "Cancellation Rate (CR) | > 17.5%".

Fraud definition (Community Guidelines version only, section 5 list): "5. Remaining stationary and uncontactable after accepting a ride to trigger cancellation or waiting fees". The same list includes "8. Abuse of Cancellation and Waiting Fee Policy". The Popular Topics version (22 May 2026) does not contain this list.

Dashcam and IVRD rule:
- Driver article "What if I have an In-Vehicle Recording Device (IVRD) including Dashcam?" (edited 19 Sep 2025): https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036735393 : "We do not allow IVRDs on the Ryde platform at this moment." "If you have IVRDs/Dashcams installed in your vehicle, you are required to remove them immediately." "Unauthorised installation of inward-facing IVRD/Dashcams may result in 21 demerit points and suspension from our platform." The matter "may also be reported to the Land Transport Authority (LTA)".
- Community Guidelines version, section 4A: "in-vehicle recording devices (IVRDs) are not allowed on the Ryde platform."
- Rider article (created 24 Oct 2025): https://help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/51812996696217 : "No. Ryde does not allow drivers to use IVRDs or dashcams at this time."
- Scope: a Ryde platform rule for drivers, covering IVRDs and dashcams generally (the removal instruction is not limited to inward-facing devices), stated as current ("at this moment", "at this time"). It is a platform policy, not a statement of Singapore law. The 21-demerit-point consequence is stated only for "Unauthorised installation of inward-facing" devices.

### Claim 11. Grab ERP and tolls on GrabCar fixed fares (note 02 vs note 08)

Verdict: CORRECTED. Note 02 is right; note 08 is wrong.

- Grab SG GrabCar page https://www.grab.com/sg/transport/car/ and transport page https://www.grab.com/sg/transport/ (undated, read 5 Oct 2026): "Know how much your trip will cost (excluding tolls and surcharges) before you book."
- Note 08's source, AsiaOne, 22 May 2023: https://www.asiaone.com/singapore/go-take-mrt-grab-driver-threatens-kick-passenger-ride-after-she-asks-choose-route-erp . No sentence in it says ERP is included in standard fares. It says (reporter's text): "When it comes to extra charges, Grab passengers are automatically charged for tolls in the app after the trip is completed". Grab on GrabHitch: "ERP fees are not included in the fare and they will typically be borne by the driver". On GrabShare: "passengers will need to pay ERP charges when the car passes through the gantry". On routes: "the driver has the right to take the fastest or shortest route available as recommended by a global positioning system."
- Grab Engineering, 31 Jul 2019: https://engineering.grab.com/automated-erp-charges : drivers previously had to enter ERP after trips and "would have had to bear the cost of the ERP fares" when they forgot; Grab introduced "automated ERP fare calculation".
